Poisoning occurs through ingestion, inhalation, injection, or dermal contact with toxic substances, pesticides, or corrosive chemicals.

## What to do immediately
1. Call Emergency Poison Control Center immediately (Mansoura Poison Center: 0502202685 or 123).
2. Identify the toxic agent, container, estimated quantity, and time of exposure.
3. Inhaled poison: Move victim into fresh air immediately while ensuring your own safety.
4. Skin contact: Strip contaminated clothing and irrigate skin with copious running water for 15 minutes.
5. Ingested aluminum phosphide (Wheat Pill / حبة الغلة): Administer paraffin oil or medicinal coconut oil immediately to coat stomach walls and block phosphine release. Seek ICU immediately.

## Do NOT
- Do NOT induce vomiting (strictly prohibited for corrosives, bleach, acids, or hydrocarbons like kerosene).
- Do NOT give water or milk for aluminum phosphide poisoning, as moisture accelerates toxic phosphine gas liberation.
- Do NOT perform mouth-to-mouth resuscitation without a protective barrier if toxins are on the victim's mouth.

## When to go to hospital
- All deliberate poisonings, pesticide exposures, and corrosive ingestions require immediate hospital admission.

## Signs of severe poisoning (RED FLAGS)
- Garlic-like odor on breath (organophosphates or phosphine).
- Pinpoint or dilated pupils, excessive salivation, and sweating.
- Convulsions, drowsiness, or respiratory failure.
